# Team Development & Team Building

## Purpose

Team development complements individual capability building by strengthening collaboration, communication, knowledge sharing and collective problem solving.

## Possible Activities

- Team-building activities
- Collaborative workshops
- Problem-solving sessions
- Knowledge-sharing sessions
- Peer learning
- Calibration
- Collective retrospectives
- Communication exercises
- Cross-functional collaboration activities
- Recognition and feedback mechanisms

## Development Objectives

Activities can support trust and communication, collaboration, role understanding, collective problem solving, knowledge sharing, ownership, team cohesion and continuous improvement.

Team-building is treated as a management and capability-development tool, not as an isolated event.
