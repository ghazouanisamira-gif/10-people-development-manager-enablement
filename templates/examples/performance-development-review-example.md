# Performance & Development Review — Example

> **Demonstration / Fictional Data — not a real employee review.**

## Review Period

Illustrative 8-week review.

## Evidence

| Area | Evidence / Trend | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| KPI / performance | Weekly target achieved inconsistently | Requires stronger trend analysis |
| Quality | Stable | No immediate development priority |
| Process adherence | Good | Maintain |
| Stakeholder impact | Positive | Maintain |
| Collaboration | Strong | Maintain |
| Autonomy | Developing | Needs structured decision practice |

## Diagnosis

Primary development causes:

- Capability: KPI interpretation
- Process: inconsistent review routine
- Measurement: actions not always linked to evidence

## Development Actions

| Action | Owner | Expected Evidence | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| KPI analysis practice | Manager | Correct diagnosis in 3 cases | Week 3 |
| Structured weekly review | Manager | Review completed independently | Week 4 |
| Coaching follow-up | Coach | Consistent action verification | Week 6 |

## Reassessment

Use observed application rather than completion of training as the primary evidence of progress.
