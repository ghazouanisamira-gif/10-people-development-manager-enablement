# Competency Matrix — Example

> **Demonstration / Fictional Data — not a real employee assessment.**

## Context

**Role:** Operations Team Leader  
**Assessment purpose:** identify development priorities during a role transition.

**Scale:** 1 Awareness → 5 Autonomy / Transfer

| Capability | Required | Current | Evidence | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Process knowledge | 4 | 3 | Can explain core workflow; needs support on exceptions | High |
| KPI management | 4 | 2 | Reviews dashboard but does not yet diagnose trends independently | High |
| Coaching | 4 | 2 | Gives feedback but lacks a structured coaching cycle | High |
| Communication | 4 | 4 | Clear and consistent in daily interactions | Maintain |
| Problem solving | 4 | 3 | Resolves routine issues; escalations remain frequent | Medium |
| Autonomy | 4 | 2 | Requires support for prioritization and escalation | High |

## Development Logic

**Evidence → Current Level → Expected Level → Gap → Root Cause → Development Action**

The assessment indicates that the main development need is not general training. It is targeted development in KPI interpretation, coaching structure and management routines.
